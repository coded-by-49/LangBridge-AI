## Models and methods

- **NLLB-200**: NLLB Team et al. (2022). *No Language Left Behind: Scaling Human-Centered Machine Translation.* [arXiv:2207.04672](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04672). Model cards: [nllb-200-distilled-1.3B](https://huggingface.co/facebook/nllb-200-distilled-1.3B), [nllb-200-distilled-600M](https://huggingface.co/facebook/nllb-200-distilled-600M).
- **LoRA**: Hu et al. (2021). [arXiv:2106.09685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685).
- **QLoRA**: Dettmers et al. (2023). *QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized LLMs.* [arXiv:2305.14314](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14314).
- **rsLoRA**: Kalajdzievski (2023). *A Rank Stabilization Scaling Factor for Fine-Tuning with LoRA.* [arXiv:2312.03732](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03732).
- **Cyclical learning rates and the LR range test**: Smith (2017). *Cyclical Learning Rates for Training Neural Networks.* WACV. [arXiv:1506.01186](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.01186).
- **Transformer**: Vaswani et al. (2017). [arXiv:1706.03762](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762).

## Evaluation

- **AfriCOMET**: Wang et al. (2024). *AfriMTE and AfriCOMET: Enhancing COMET to Embrace Under-resourced African Languages.* NAACL. [ACL Anthology 2024.naacl-long.334](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.334/). Checkpoint: [masakhane/africomet-mtl](https://huggingface.co/masakhane/africomet-mtl).
- **COMET**: Rei et al. (2020). *COMET: A Neural Framework for MT Evaluation.* EMNLP. [arXiv:2009.09025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.09025).
- **Paired bootstrap resampling**: Koehn (2004). *Statistical Significance Tests for Machine Translation Evaluation.* EMNLP. [ACL Anthology W04-3250](https://aclanthology.org/W04-3250/).
- **chrF++**: Popović (2017). *chrF++: words helping character n-grams.* WMT. [ACL Anthology W17-4770](https://aclanthology.org/W17-4770/).
- **BLEU**: Papineni et al. (2002). *BLEU: a Method for Automatic Evaluation of Machine Translation.* ACL. [ACL Anthology P02-1040](https://aclanthology.org/P02-1040/).
